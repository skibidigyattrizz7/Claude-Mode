# Feature ideas

Ordered by how much each one adds for the effort. ✅ means it's already in the current build.

## Already added
- ✅ Goal replays
- ✅ Skill moves
- ✅ Local multiplayer
- ✅ World Cup tournament (2D)
- ✅ Career mode and Ultimate Team (3D)
- ✅ Online 1v1 (3D)

## Gameplay depth
1. **Contextual first touch.** How clean your touch is depends on ball speed, your dribbling stat and whether you're sprinting. Heavy touches give defenders a chance to win the ball, so passing speed matters.
2. **Jockey / contain button.** Hold it to shadow the attacker without diving in. Tackling becomes a timing decision instead of button mashing.
3. **Team tactics slider.** Set pressing intensity, defensive line height and width. The AI actually changes shape (high press or low block).
4. **Player instructions.** For example "stay back", "get in behind" or "cut inside". This makes squad choices matter in Career and UT.
5. **Timed finishing.** Tap shoot a second time on contact for extra accuracy. Mistime it and you get more error. This adds a skill ceiling.
6. **Weather.** Rain makes the ball skid, snow slows it down and wind shifts curve. It changes how you play each match.
7. **Fatigue and injuries in a match.** Late in the game, tired players are slower and more error-prone, so substitutions matter.

## Modes
8. **Daily challenge.** A fixed seed gives everyone the same scenario, like "down 1–0 with 10 minutes left". Beat it for UT coins.
9. **Skill games.** Passing gates, dribble slaloms and crossbar challenges, with leaderboards. They double as practice.
10. **Online ranked seasons.** Divisions, promotion points and seasonal rewards. This needs a small backend for match results.
11. **Draft mode (UT).** Pick 1 of 5 players for each position, then play a 4-match run for rewards.
12. **Spectate / watch AI vs AI.** Useful for testing, and fun to watch.

## Presentation
13. **Commentary.** Text or speech-synthesised lines for goals, saves and near misses using the Web Speech API. It's free and adds a lot of atmosphere.
14. **Kit editor.** Design your UT club's kit and crest.
15. **Stadium upgrades (UT).** Spend coins on stands, lighting and pitch patterns.
16. **Photo mode.** Pause during a replay and move the camera freely.

## Technical
17. **Save sync.** Export and import your saves as a code or file so you can move them between devices.
18. **PWA.** Install it to your home screen and play offline on your phone.
19. **Accessibility.** Colour-blind kit indicators, and slow-motion or auto-pass assists for new players.
