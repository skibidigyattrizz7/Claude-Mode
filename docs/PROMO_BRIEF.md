# Promo card look — owner's reference images (FUT 17 / FIFA 19 / FC 26 style)

Reference images (local only): scratchpad `promorefs/` (TOTS team sheet, colour-variant sheet, card-template sheet, big FIFA 19 promo collage, FIFA20 vs FC26 promo list). Owner will send more.

## Card anatomy (every card)
- Shield shape with notched/curved top corners (not a plain rectangle), thin metallic border.
- Top-left column: big rating, position under it, then nation flag and club crest stacked.
- Player art on the right/centre, **overlapping the frame** (head can break out of the top edge on special cards).
- Name bar across the middle, then 6 stats in two columns (PAC SHO PAS | DRI DEF PHY), with numbers bold and labels light.
- Special cards: art background (gradients, light streaks, geometric shards, halftone/foil texture), not flat colour.

## Base tiers
Bronze / silver / gold, each common (matte) and rare (shiny + pattern). In-form = black with gold/white text. Icons = cream/white with gold trim, sepia-feel photo area.

## Promo palette ideas (each promo = own card + pack theme)
- Season best (TOTS-like): deep royal blue, gold trim, gold numbers.
- Summer party (Futties-like): hot pink → purple gradient, light-pink numbers.
- Future stars: electric blue + lightning streaks.
- Champions: purple + gold.
- Halloween (Scream-like): orange + black.
- Rulebreakers/shapeshifters: split two-colour diagonal (red/white, teal/navy).
- Winter: white/ice blue with snowflake pattern. Christmas: red + green.
- Birthday: multicolour confetti. Flashback: faded sepia + colour accent.
- Road to the final: dark navy + star pattern. Heroes: dark with gold halo.
- Thunderstruck-like: black + neon lightning. Trailblazers: fire orange gradient.
FC26 promo list to draw names from (use generic/alt names, no EA trademarks): Centurions, Pitch Beats, Shapeshifters, Thunderstruck, Team of the Week, Rulebreakers, Road to the Knockouts, Trailblazers, Rush, Winter Wildcards, Birthday, Fantasy, Future Stars, Team of the Season, Flashback, Best Of, European Dreams, Grassroots Greats, Ones to Watch, Heroes, Summer party, Pre-Season, Future Icons, Iconic Moments, Black Friday, Total Rush, Player of the Month, End of an Era.

## Rules
Procedural (CSS gradients/SVG patterns/canvas), no copied EA art or logos. Cards must stay light and fast in squad/market grids (one CSS class per promo + shared shape). Pack-opening theme colours come from the same promo definition.
